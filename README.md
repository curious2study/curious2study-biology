# Biology Course - Curious2Study

## Course Overview

This biology course covers general biology from the chemistry of life through ecology and conservation. It is built for high school and lower-division undergraduate students, with a focus on clear explanations and practical study strategies.

## Course Materials

Each chapter includes:
- **Video Lectures:** Explanations of core concepts with visual demonstrations
- **Study Guides:** Chapter summaries and key terms

## How to Use This Course

1. **Start with Chapter 1** to build a foundation in biological principles
2. **Watch the video lectures** for each section
3. **Read the study guides** to reinforce key ideas
4. **Focus on understanding** how systems connect across units, from molecules to ecosystems, rather than memorizing terms in isolation

## Learning Approach

This course emphasizes:
- Building a solid conceptual foundation across cell biology, genetics, evolution, and ecology
- Recognizing patterns that repeat across scales, from molecular pathways to whole ecosystems
- Connecting biology to real-world applications in health, agriculture, and conservation
- Practicing study habits that support long-term retention

## Prerequisites

- Basic chemistry concepts, such as atoms, molecules, and reactions (helpful, not required)
- Comfort with reading diagrams and interpreting data
- No calculus or advanced math required
